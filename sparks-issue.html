Newsletter of the Tri-State Amateur Radio Society

In This Issue

The February 2026 issue of SPARKS, newsletter of the Tri-State Amateur Radio Society, is now available. Download the full PDF from the attachments panel or read the highlights below.

🍽 Annual Banquet — February 12th

The TARS regular February meeting is this Thursday, Feb 12th at 5:30 PM at Golden Corral. This is our annual banquet — see the full details in the attached SPARKS PDF!

📝 VE Testing — February 28th

The next VE testing session is Saturday, February 28th at 9:00 AM Central Time at the Evansville Red Cross, 29 S Stockwell Rd. Pre-register at HamStudy.org.

⚠️ 2026 Dues Are Now Past Due!

If you haven't renewed your TARS membership for 2026, please do so as soon as possible. A 2026 membership application form is attached below.

Stay Connected

For the latest TARS news between newsletters, check in to the Tri-State Emergency Net on the 146.79 repeater (PL 88.5), every Wednesday at 8:00 PM Central Time.

Also join the 10m Ragchew Net on 28.355 MHz (±) at 8:30 PM, or following the conclusion of the 2m net.

Follow us on Facebook for real-time updates, photos from club events, and discussions with fellow members.
