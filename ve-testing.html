What to Bring

Exam day checklist

Whether you're testing for your first license or upgrading, here's everything you need.

  • ✓FCC FRN Number — All candidates must have a Federal Registration Number. Register at FCC CORES if you don't have one.
  • ✓Two forms of ID — One must bear a recent photograph (driver's license, passport, etc.)
  • ✓Testing fee: $15 cash — Under 18: $5. Cash only.
  • ✓Copy of current license — If upgrading, bring a copy of your existing license.
  • ✓Any CSCE documents — Original Credit for Successful Completion of Examination documents, if applicable.

Online Exams

Exams are administered online using provided laptops. You may request to use a personal device (laptop, tablet, or smartphone), subject to VE Team approval and a device audit.

Need Your FRN?

If you currently hold an FCC license, you already have an FRN. If not, register at the FCC CORES system — you'll be issued an FRN upon completing registration.

Questions?

Contact the VE team at vetesting@w9og.net for any questions about sessions, requirements, or registration.
