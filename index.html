About TARS

More than a hobby — it's a community service

Founded February 6, 1947, TARS is an ARRL Special Service Club dedicated to advancing amateur radio and serving the Evansville tri-state area through emergency communications, public service events, and education.

🛰

ARRL Affiliated

Recognized Special Service Club with strong national ties

📡

Emergency Ready

🎓

Licensing Classes

Regular classes and VE testing for new and upgrading hams

What We Do

On the air and in the community

From weekly nets to annual hamfests, TARS keeps the airwaves alive with activity, learning, and camaraderie.

📻

Weekly Emergency Net

Join the Tri-State Emergency Net every Wednesday at 8 PM CT on 146.79 MHz (PL 88.5). Stay connected, stay informed.

🎪

HamtoberFest

Our annual hamfest every October — swap equipment, attend forums, and connect with hams from across the region.

⚡

Field Day

ARRL's premier operating event. TARS stations have earned top national rankings, including 4th place in the 3A Battery category.

🌪

Skywarn Weather Spotting

Working with the National Weather Service, our operators provide critical real-time severe weather data for public safety.

🎓

License Testing & Classes

Studying for your Technician, General, or Extra? We offer classes and coordinate VE testing sessions throughout the year.

🤝

Community Events

From marathons to parades, TARS provides volunteer communications support for community events across the tri-state area.
