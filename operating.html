Operating Activities

From Field Day to special event stations, TARS members stay active on the air year-round with contests, QSO parties, and community events.

Annual Events

On the air with TARS

Our major operating events bring members together for camaraderie, skill-building, and top-tier contest performance.

📡

Field Day

ARRL's premier annual emergency preparedness exercise. TARS operates a multi-station setup and has placed 4th nationally in the 3A Battery category. A great event for both experienced operators and newcomers to get hands-on experience.

June ARRL Contest All Welcome
❄️

Winter Field Day

Cold-weather operating event held in January. Setup, operate, and enjoy warm soup and camaraderie. A perfect complement to the summer event and great practice for winter emergency preparedness.

January Contest Outdoors
🏁

Indiana QSO Party

Statewide contest with CW and SSB stations. TARS sets up a full operating location — a great opportunity for new hams to experience contesting with hands-on antenna and station setup.

May State QSO Party CW & SSB
🎪

HamtoberFest

TARS's annual hamfest held every October. Swap meet, vendor tables, forums, prize drawings, and food. A highlight of the tri-state ham radio calendar.

October Hamfest Open to Public
Special Event Stations

On-air activations

TARS participates in special event activations that celebrate history, community, and the amateur radio hobby.

Lincoln Special Event

Commemorating Abraham Lincoln's connection to the tri-state area with a special on-air activation.

Audubon Special Event — W4A

Celebrating the legacy of John James Audubon with special event operations under the W4A callsign.

Lewis & Clark Trail on the Air

W9OG is one of twenty activating stations across 16 states in the LCTOTA special event. All contacts uploaded to LoTW.

Indiana Parks on the Air

Portable operations from Indiana state parks — combining the outdoors with amateur radio activations.

Boy Scout Jamboree on the Air

Youth outreach event connecting scouts with amateur radio operators worldwide.

Annual Banquet

February celebration featuring awards, guest speakers, and recognition of outstanding member contributions.

Special Interest Groups

Dive deeper

TARS supports focused interest groups for members who want to explore specific aspects of amateur radio.

📦

Packet Group

Digital packet radio communications, BBS systems, and data networking over amateur frequencies.

🔗

HSMM MESH Group

High-Speed Multimedia mesh networking for emergency and community communications infrastructure.

📍

APRS Group

Automatic Packet Reporting System for real-time position tracking, weather stations, and tactical communication.
